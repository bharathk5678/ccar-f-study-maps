# Suggested study plan

Weights follow the unofficial study guide, so verify against the official exam guide.

| Domain | Weight | Suggested share of time |
|--------|:------:|:-----------------------:|
| 1 Agentic Architecture & Orchestration | 27% | ~27% |
| 3 Claude Code Configuration & Workflows | 20% | ~20% |
| 4 Prompt Engineering & Structured Output | 20% | ~20% |
| 2 Tool Design & MCP Integration | 18% | ~18% |
| 5 Context Management & Reliability | 15% | ~15% |

## Daily loop for each domain
1. **Skim the Coverage table** (5 min): know what is in scope.
2. **Read two or three panels** (25 min): say each core idea out loud in one sentence.
3. **Traps and discriminators** (15 min): cover the answer, predict the right choice.
4. **Flashcards** (10 min): anything you miss goes in your own "weak spots" note.
5. **Scenario Q&A** (15 min): explain why each wrong option is wrong.

## Final week
- Re-do traps and scenario Q&A for the weakest two domains.
- Check every `VERIFY` item against the official course and docs.
- Do a mixed round across all five domains.
